Marketing copy from a local LLM, from typed facts or a product photo

Source code (opens in a new tab)

About the project

A team project with Maksymilian Przypek: ad headlines, slogans, product descriptions and social posts in Polish and English, written by a local LLM (Qwen 2.5 7B) through Ollama in three brand voices, with structured output checked against a JSON schema. A vision model describes a product photo for the copy, whole catalogues and campaigns are generated from a CSV, and every piece passes automatic checks for length, keyword, forbidden phrases, language and hashtags. A Gradio web app and a Typer CLI share one core, and Docker Compose starts everything.

What it demonstrates

  • Three brand voices in two languages
  • Copy from a product photo
  • Batch catalogue from a CSV
  • Automatic quality checks
  • Parameter experiment
